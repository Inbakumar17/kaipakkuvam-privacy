Kaipakkuvam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Kaipakkuvam is a cooking companion app for Tamil Nadu home food, created by Inbakumar Rajagopal. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Almost everything stays on your phone.

Information kept only on your phone

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, phone number or email, weight and height. These are used to greet you and to work out your diet plan.
  • Your settings, cooking progress, timers, pantry list, events, shopping lists and diet plans.
  • All of this is stored on your phone only. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Feedback you choose to send

  • When you tap Send on the feedback or "report a problem" screen, the app sends: the reason you picked, the words you typed, the dish (if the report is about a recipe), the app version, your phone's system type and version (for example "android 34"), the app language and the time.
  • Your name and phone number or email are added only if you tick "Add my name and phone or email so the team can reply to me". It is off by default.
  • Feedback is sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection to a private Google Sheet and email inbox that only the developer can see, using Google Apps Script. If you are offline, it waits on your phone and is sent the next time you open the app.
  • Feedback is used only to fix problems and improve the app. It is kept until it is no longer needed, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Location (optional)

  • If you allow location, the app uses your approximate location, only while the app is open, to check whether it is raining near you so it can suggest rainy-day snacks.
  • The location is rounded to about 1 km before it is used. The rounded position is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather service, to get the forecast. It is not sent to us and not stored.
  • The app never uses your location in the background. You can say no, or turn it off later in Settings; everything else keeps working.

Microphone and voice (optional)

  • The microphone is used only while you hold the mic button, to turn your words into text (for example a dish name).
  • This uses your phone's own speech service (on most Android phones, Google's). The app does not record or keep any audio. Google's privacy policy covers its speech service.
  • Reading recipe steps aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech and sends nothing anywhere.

Notifications and alarms

  • Cooking timers, tea-time, meal-time and event reminders are set up and shown on your phone only. No push service or server is involved.

Other apps

  • If you tap a music app (such as Spotify or YouTube Music) or "Rate us on Google Play", the app simply opens that app or website. Nothing about you is sent with it. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Children

  • Kaipakkuvam is a general cooking app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us feedback with personal details, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time, refuse or turn off any permission in your phone's settings, and delete all data by uninstalling the app.
  • To ask what feedback we hold from you, or to have it deleted, email us. We will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
